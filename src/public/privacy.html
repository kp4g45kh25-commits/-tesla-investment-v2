PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

This independent prototype is designed to demonstrate account-management functionality. It is not intended to collect or process real financial information.

Information used by the prototype

Demo account information may include a name, email address, account status and fictional portfolio data.

Passwords

In a properly deployed version, passwords should be securely hashed and never stored as plain text. Users should not reuse passwords from other services.

Financial information

Do not enter real bank-account numbers, payment-card details, cryptocurrency private keys, government identification numbers or other sensitive financial information into this prototype.

Cookies and sessions

A production implementation may use secure session cookies for authentication. Cookies should be configured with appropriate security controls before deployment.

Third-party services

This prototype does not itself provide banking, brokerage, payment-processing or cryptocurrency custody services.

Data security

Any production deployment should use HTTPS, secure authentication, access controls, encrypted secrets, database protection, logging and appropriate security monitoring.

Tesla relationship

Tesla Investment is an independent prototype and is not Tesla, Inc. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.

Contact and legal review

Before using this software to provide real financial services or collect sensitive customer information, obtain appropriate legal, regulatory, privacy and cybersecurity review.
